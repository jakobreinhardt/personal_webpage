About Me

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair of Ergonomics, Technical University of Munich (TUM), where I lead the Robotics For Life and Healthcare research group. My work focuses on Human Factors in Robotic Assisted Surgery, bringing together my expertise in data analytics, user research, and human-robot interaction.

Previously, I worked as a Data Analyst at Corpuls, a leading medical device company, where I developed data analysis concepts for post-market clinical follow-up studies using real-world rescue mission data.

Outside of work, I'm an active member of Bergwacht Bayern (Bavarian Mountain Rescue), serving as a paramedic. I'm passionate about paragliding, ski mountaineering, and spending time in the mountains with my family.
